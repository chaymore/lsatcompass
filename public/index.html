  3. Keep a wrong answer journalWrite down every miss, so the same mistakes stop coming back.

    For every question you miss, or guess on, write down:

    • The question: the test, section and number, or a screenshot
    • The answer you picked, and why it was tempting
    • Why the right answer is right
    • How you'll get it right next time

    A week later, redo each question without looking at your notes. Every week, look for the mistakes that keep showing up, and drill those question types.

    Spreadsheet One row per question. Easy to sort and filter. Opens in Google Sheets, Excel or Numbers. Document One box per question, with room to paste the question. Opens in Google Docs, Word or Pages.
