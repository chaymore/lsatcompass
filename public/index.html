Take a free official test

LSAC writes the LSAT. LSAC gives you 4 free past tests on LawHub. Take one test to get your starting score.

  1. Make a free account at lawhub.org.
  2. Open the LSAT Prep library.
  3. Select a free PrepTest.
  4. Take the full test in one session, with the timer on.
  5. Write down your scaled score and the number correct in each section.
  6. Come back here and enter your score.
